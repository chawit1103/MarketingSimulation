# Sample Demo Briefs

These sample briefs are synthetic demo data only. They are intended for local demos, sales walkthroughs, and controlled private pilot discovery. They must not be presented as real customer data, live market evidence, production-readiness evidence, or guaranteed predictions.

Use these briefs with visible source/provenance labels and the demo artifact checklist.

## Premium Water Launch

Synthetic demo data only.

| Field | Brief |
| --- | --- |
| Objective | Pre-test a premium bottled water launch message before wider media spend. |
| Target segment | Urban professionals, health-conscious shoppers, premium convenience-store buyers. |
| Market context | Crowded hydration category with strong price sensitivity and skepticism around premium claims. |
| Channels | TikTok, Instagram, convenience-store retail media, creator short-form video. |
| Budget band | Medium pilot budget; enough for creator testing and a limited retail activation. |
| Risk concerns | Greenwashing claims, plastic packaging criticism, price backlash, vague health-benefit wording. |
| Competitor context | Mainstream water brands compete on price and availability; premium imported brands compete on status. |
| Validation goal | Identify which claim, proof point, and segment should be validated in a controlled audience test. |

## Usage Notes

- Do not add customer-specific facts, real budgets, real audience records, or real campaign performance.
- Do not claim exact ROI/ROAS prediction.
- Do not claim production readiness, public pilot readiness, or public internet readiness.
