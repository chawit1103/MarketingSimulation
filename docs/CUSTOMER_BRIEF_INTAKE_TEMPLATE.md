## Approval

- [ ] The customer confirms this brief or historical campaign scenario is approved for proof-of-value pilot use.
- [ ] The customer confirms the brief includes no PII.
- [ ] The customer confirms the brief includes no raw CRM records.
- [ ] The customer confirms the brief includes no customer lists.
- [ ] The customer confirms the brief includes no account-level, household-level, or individual-level customer records.
- [ ] The customer confirms the brief includes no API keys, provider tokens, OAuth credentials, passwords, graph credentials, or other secrets.
- [ ] The customer confirms no live CRM, social-listening, ad-platform, analytics, or marketing automation integration is required for the session.
- [ ] The customer confirms any historical actuals are aggregate-only and approved for pilot use.

## Operator Notes

- Replace specific customer, client, or campaign labels with an anonymized campaign code if the customer has not approved the real label for pilot use.
- Use broad budget bands instead of exact financial details unless exact figures are explicitly approved.
- Keep the original approved intake record in the agreed pilot storage location only.
- Do not paste raw briefs, secrets, or customer data into issue comments, screenshots, chat, or public documents.
