# Proof-of-Value Sales Offer

Updated: 2026-05-17

This offer is a customer-facing proof-of-value workshop/session for teams that want to evaluate 3C Simulator with one approved campaign brief or historical scenario. It is positioned as a controlled private pilot candidate and decision-support estimate, not production SaaS.

## Offer Name

3C Simulator Proof-of-Value Workshop

## Target Customer

Brand, marketing, agency, PR, communications, and strategy teams that need a fast decision-support read before committing campaign budget or choosing a creative direction.

Good fit:

- A team has one campaign brief or historical campaign scenario approved for pilot use.
- The team wants to compare creative directions before a larger test.
- The team wants a structured risk/watchout view before launch.
- The team can work without PII, raw CRM records, customer lists, secrets, or live integrations.

## Problem It Solves

Teams often have a campaign brief, multiple creative directions, and limited time to identify:

- whether the brief is complete enough to test,
- which creative direction is strongest,
- which risks or crisis watchouts need attention,
- how to turn a rough brief into a clearer Revised Brief v2,
- what should be validated next through normal research, media, analytics, or post-campaign measurement.

The workshop gives the customer a concrete proof-of-value package using approved business context while preserving a clear data boundary.

## Customer Input Required

The customer provides:

- one approved campaign brief or approved historical campaign scenario,
- campaign name or anonymized campaign code,
- product/service,
- objective,
- target segment,
- market context,
- channel plan,
- budget band,
- competitor context,
- creative directions A/B/C if available,
- risk concerns,
- success KPIs,
- optional approved aggregate actuals if historical.

The customer must confirm:

- the brief is approved for proof-of-value use,
- no PII is included,
- no raw CRM records are included,
- no customer lists are included,
- no secrets are included,
- no live integrations are required,
- optional actuals are aggregate-only and approved if provided.

## Deliverables

The customer receives a proof-of-value output package that may include:

- one-page executive summary,
- Brief Quality Finding,
- Campaign Simulation Summary,
- A/B/C Creative Comparison when creative directions are provided,
- Risk and Crisis Watchouts,
- Revised Brief v2,
- Strategy Pack PPTX slide outline or export,
- optional Actual vs Estimate comparison when approved aggregate actuals are provided,
- Recommended Validation Step,
- Limitations and Assumptions.

Each deliverable should preserve visible source/provenance labels and decision-support estimate language.

## Session Format

Recommended format:

- 30-minute proof-of-value walkthrough for a sales conversation.
- 60-minute proof-of-value workshop for a deeper customer review.

Typical participants:

- customer marketing or brand lead,
- agency or strategy lead if relevant,
- 3C operator/facilitator,
- optional decision maker for paid pilot scoping.

## Timeline

Example timeline:

| Step | Timing |
| --- | --- |
| Intake and safety review | 1-2 business days before session |
| Proof-of-value walkthrough | 30 or 60 minutes |
| Output package follow-up | Same day or next business day |
| Paid pilot scoping decision | Within 1 week if the customer wants to continue |

Timing depends on customer intake completeness and operator availability.

## Data Boundary

Allowed:

- one approved campaign brief or approved historical scenario,
- anonymized campaign code or approved campaign name,
- aggregate-only actuals if approved,
- synthetic/demo examples for explanation.

Not allowed:

- PII,
- raw CRM records,
- customer lists,
- account-level, household-level, or individual-level records,
- API keys, provider tokens, OAuth credentials, passwords, graph credentials, private keys, or customer-owned secrets,
- live CRM, social-listening, ad-platform, analytics, or marketing automation integrations,
- screenshots, decks, or exports that expose unsafe data.

## What Is Excluded

This offer does not include:

- production deployment,
- public pilot access,
- public internet exposure,
- live CRM/social-listening/ad-platform integrations,
- ingestion of PII, raw CRM records, customer lists, or secrets,
- guaranteed prediction,
- exact ROI or ROAS prediction,
- replacement for market research,
- binary PPTX files in the repository.

## Success Criteria

The workshop is successful if the customer can answer:

- Does this output help clarify the campaign decision?
- Which creative direction is worth testing or revising?
- Which risk or watchout should be addressed before launch?
- Is the Revised Brief v2 useful enough to review internally?
- Is there a clear next validation step?
- Is a controlled paid pilot worth scoping?

Success should be measured by decision usefulness and clarity of next validation, not guaranteed performance outcomes.

## Next Step CTA

Recommended close:

> If this proof-of-value package helps your team make a clearer campaign decision, the next step is a controlled paid pilot scoping call. We will agree on one campaign question, approved data inputs, output format, access controls, retention/deletion ownership, and validation criteria before any customer data is used.
