## Risk and Crisis Watchouts

Risk drivers:

- Greenwashing accusations if sustainability claims are broad or unsupported.
- Pricing confusion if the offer does not explain billing, cancellation, and service continuity.
- Reliability concerns if the campaign implies a service change without explaining support coverage.

Crisis watchouts:

- Social comments asking whether the offer is a hidden fee increase.
- Local media questions about whether the energy source claims are independently verified.
- Customer-support spikes around billing terms after paid media launch.

Mitigation:

- Add plain-language proof points and terms before launch.
- Prepare a response guide for reliability, billing, and sustainability questions.
- Monitor support themes and community feedback during the first two weeks.
