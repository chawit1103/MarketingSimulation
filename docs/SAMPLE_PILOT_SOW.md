# Sample Pilot SOW

Updated: 2026-05-17

This is a practical template, not legal advice. A customer-facing SOW should be reviewed by the business owner and counsel before use.

## Project

Controlled 3C Simulator pilot for campaign, competitor, or crisis scenario planning.

## Objective

Help the customer evaluate approved campaign or strategy scenarios using synthetic persona simulation, structured evidence, and decision-support recommendations.

Outputs are estimates for planning and validation. They are not guaranteed predictions.

## Scope

Included:

- Pilot kickoff and discovery.
- Up to 1-3 approved scenario briefs.
- Brief Quality Score review.
- Demo Mode walkthrough.
- Scenario dashboard review.
- War Room scenario review when relevant.
- Structured Action Plan.
- Strategy Pack PPTX export.
- Pilot exit review.

Excluded:

- Production SaaS deployment.
- Public internet exposure.
- Live CRM, ad-platform, or social-listening integration.
- Raw PII, raw CRM records, or customer lists.
- API keys, provider tokens, OAuth credentials, passwords, graph credentials, or customer-owned provider secrets entered through browser fields.
- Guaranteed ROI, revenue, conversion, crisis, or market-share prediction.
- Custom model training.

## Customer Responsibilities

The customer will:

- Provide approved, anonymized, non-PII campaign briefs.
- Identify a pilot sponsor and pilot lead.
- Confirm data-use approval before upload or entry.
- Review assumptions, limitations, and source labels.
- Provide feedback on usefulness, trust, and next-step decisions.

## Provider Responsibilities

The provider will:

- Configure the controlled pilot environment.
- Keep source/provenance labels visible.
- Avoid storing or exposing raw secrets.
- Run agreed scenarios.
- Present findings and limitations.
- Deliver the final pilot pack and exit recommendation.

## Timeline

Example 3-week pilot:

- Week 1: discovery, data approval, scenario setup.
- Week 2: simulations, dashboard review, War Room review.
- Week 3: strategy pack, decision workshop, exit review.

## Deliverables

- Pilot scenario register.
- Brief Quality Score summary.
- Source-labeled dashboard summaries.
- Action Plan.
- Strategy Pack PPTX.
- Pilot Success Criteria scorecard.
- Pilot Exit Checklist.

## Data Handling

The first pilot will not use PII, raw CRM data, live integrations, provider credentials supplied through the browser, or customer secrets. If customer-owned credentials are ever needed in a later approved phase, they must be configured only through an approved deployment secret path, environment variable, or secret manager. Data-handling rules are defined in [CUSTOMER_DATA_HANDLING.md](CUSTOMER_DATA_HANDLING.md).
