# Founder First Customer Close Pack

Updated: 2026-05-18

Use this pack when the founder or operator is personally selling the first customer Proof-of-Value Workshop and trying to convert it into a controlled paid pilot. The goal is a practical close path: find the right buyer, qualify fast, run one approved campaign brief through `/proof-of-value`, deliver a useful output package, and ask for a scoped paid pilot.

This pack does not approve production SaaS, public pilot access, public internet exposure, live integrations, or self-serve customer trial access.

## Ideal First Customer Profile

Best first customers have a real campaign decision and enough urgency to value a guided operator-led session:

- brand, agency, PR, communications, or strategy team,
- campaign decision due within 30-60 days,
- one approved campaign brief or historical scenario available,
- willingness to work without PII, raw CRM records, customer lists, secrets, or live integrations,
- clear interest in creative direction, risk, message quality, or validation,
- ability to attend a 30-60 minute guided session with the founder/operator.

## Best First Buyer Personas

| Persona | Why they buy | Best angle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marketing Manager / Brand Lead | Needs to choose a campaign direction before spend is committed. | "Pressure-test one approved brief and identify what to validate next." |
| Agency Strategy Lead | Needs a sharper client recommendation or pitch narrative. | "Turn one client-approved brief into a structured strategy package." |
| PR / Communications Lead | Needs risk, crisis, and message watchouts before launch. | "Surface risk drivers and tighten the narrative before exposure." |
| Founder/CEO with active campaign pressure | Needs a fast decision on positioning, launch, or spend. | "Get a decision-support read on one campaign question next week." |

## Qualification Criteria

Move forward if the prospect:

- has a campaign decision within 30-60 days,
- has one approved campaign brief or historical scenario,
- can work without PII, raw CRM records, customer lists, secrets, or live integrations,
- cares about creative direction, risk, or validation,
- can attend a 30-60 minute guided session,
- can name the person who will judge whether the output is useful,
- accepts source/provenance labels and limitations in customer-facing outputs.

## Disqualification Criteria

Do not push the first-customer offer if the prospect:

- wants self-serve trial access immediately,
- requires raw CRM, PII, customer lists, or live integrations,
- expects exact ROI/ROAS prediction,
- wants production SaaS deployment now,
- cannot approve a campaign brief for workshop use,
- will not accept limitations, source/provenance labels, or a validation step,
- cannot attend the guided session or provide a decision owner.

## Default Offer

Offer name: **Founder Proof-of-Value Workshop**

Recommended first-customer package:

- 60-minute guided workshop,
- 1 approved campaign brief or approved historical scenario,
- `/proof-of-value` intake and safety confirmation,
- customer-facing output package by the next business day,
- one follow-up call,
- optional credit toward a controlled paid pilot if the customer proceeds within an agreed decision window.

Position it as a founder-led decision-support workshop, not a product trial or production deployment.

## Founder CTA

Use one of these direct calls to action:

- "Send one approved brief or two 30-minute slots."
- "I can run one founder-led PoV session next week; Tue/Wed work?"
- "If this helps clarify the campaign decision, we can scope a controlled paid pilot afterward."
- "If it is not useful, we stop after the output review."

## Close Sequence

1. Send outreach.
2. Qualify for urgency, buyer, brief availability, and data boundary fit.
3. Request one approved brief using [Customer Brief Intake Template](CUSTOMER_BRIEF_INTAKE_TEMPLATE.md).
4. Run `/proof-of-value` and resolve every missing approval or unsafe intake signal.
5. Assemble the output package using [Proof-of-Value Customer Output Template](PROOF_OF_VALUE_CUSTOMER_OUTPUT_TEMPLATE.md).
6. Review with the customer in a guided session or follow-up call.
7. Ask whether to scope a controlled paid pilot.

## Call Flow

Use this simple founder-led call structure:

| Time | Step | Founder prompt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-5 min | Qualify the decision | "What campaign decision do you need to make in the next 30-60 days?" |
| 5-10 min | Confirm brief availability | "Do you have one approved brief or historical scenario we can use without disallowed data?" |
| 10-15 min | Confirm data boundary | "Can we keep this to approved campaign context, no PII, raw CRM, customer lists, secrets, or live integrations?" |
| 15-20 min | Explain the package | "You get brief quality, simulation summary, A/B/C comparison, risks, Revised Brief v2, Strategy Pack outline, and validation step." |
| 20-25 min | Name the offer | "The recommended first step is the Founder Proof-of-Value Workshop." |
| 25-30 min | Close the next action | "Send one approved brief or two 30-minute slots. I can run one founder-led PoV session next week; Tue/Wed work?" |

## Post-Call Next Step

After a qualified call, send:

- Proof-of-Value one-page copy,
- brief intake template,
- sample proof-of-value package,
- proposed workshop time,
- data boundary reminder,
- pricing placeholder or commercial note if appropriate.

If the prospect wants to continue, book a paid pilot scoping call to define:

- pilot question,
- approved data inputs,
- access controls,
- retention/deletion owner,
- success criteria,
- customer-side decision date,
- artifact sharing boundary.

## Controlled Paid Pilot Close

Ask this at the end of the output review:

> Did this package help clarify a real campaign decision enough to scope a controlled paid pilot?

If yes, move to a paid pilot scoping call. Do not promise production deployment, public access, exact ROI/ROAS, or guaranteed outcomes.
