# Proof-of-Value Deliverables

Updated: 2026-05-17

This document defines what a customer may receive after a proof-of-value session using one approved campaign brief or approved historical campaign scenario. Deliverables are for decision support and pilot evaluation only.

## Deliverable Set

| Deliverable | Expected format | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brief Quality Score | In-app view or exported summary | Identify brief clarity, missing context, risk signals, and validation needs. |
| Campaign Simulation Summary | In-app view, Markdown summary, or report excerpt | Summarize segment reactions, likely friction, and directional campaign implications. |
| A/B/C Creative Comparator result | In-app comparator view or summary table | Compare approved creative directions against target segment and risk considerations. |
| Risk and Crisis Watchouts | In-app view or slide summary | Highlight narrative risks, sensitivity concerns, and monitoring priorities. |
| Revised Brief v2 | Markdown or report section | Provide a revised decision-support brief that the customer can review and validate. |
| Strategy Pack PPTX | PPTX export | Package executive decision summary, segment reactions, risk drivers, action plan, and limitations. |
| Optional Actual vs Estimate comparison | Summary table or short report section | Compare aggregate historical actuals against estimates when approved aggregate actuals are provided. |

## Required Source And Provenance Labels

Every deliverable should preserve visible source/provenance labels where applicable:

- Demo Mode,
- Local Estimate,
- Live Backend,
- Backend Verified,
- Unknown Source.

Strategy Pack PPTX exports should include a footer with:

- source mode,
- data basis,
- safe run/demo ID if available,
- `Decision-support estimate, not a guaranteed prediction`.

## Limitations

Proof-of-value deliverables:

- are decision-support estimates,
- depend on the quality and completeness of the approved brief,
- should be validated against the customer's normal research, media, analytics, and post-campaign measurement processes,
- should not be treated as certain forecasts or replacement market research.

## Recommended Validation Step

Before moving to a controlled paid pilot, agree on one or two validation questions, such as:

- Which creative direction should be tested first?
- Which segment response or risk signal should be validated through research?
- Which revised brief recommendation is worth taking into campaign planning?
- Which aggregate actuals would be useful for a later Actual vs Estimate comparison?

## Not Included

The proof-of-value package does not include:

- guaranteed prediction,
- exact ROI or ROAS prediction,
- live CRM integration,
- live social-listening integration,
- live ad-platform integration,
- production deployment,
- public pilot access,
- public internet exposure,
- ingestion of PII, raw CRM records, customer lists, or individual-level customer data.
