# Pilot Discovery Questions

Updated: 2026-05-17

Use these questions before agreeing to a paid pilot. The goal is to confirm business value, data safety, and buyer expectations.

## Business Context

- What decision do you need to make in the next 30-60 days?
- Is the decision about campaign launch, message revision, competitor response, crisis prevention, or client pitch support?
- Who owns the final decision?
- What happens if the decision is wrong?
- What would make this pilot worth paying for?

## Scenario Scope

- Which campaign, product, issue, or competitor move should we simulate?
- Which market or region matters?
- Which audience segments are most important?
- Which channels matter most?
- What competitor context should be included?
- What regulatory, legal, cultural, or brand constraints matter?

## Data Safety

- Can the scenario be described without PII?
- Can you provide anonymized or approved campaign briefs only?
- Are any inputs confidential, regulated, or customer-identifying?
- Who can approve pilot data use?
- Are screenshots, recordings, or exported decks allowed?

## Success Criteria

- What action should the pilot help you decide: launch, revise, hold, validate, or pitch?
- Which output would you show leadership or a client?
- What confidence threshold would make the output useful?
- What would make the output not credible?
- What validation step would you still need after the simulation?

## Buyer And Commercial Fit

- Who would sponsor a paid pilot?
- Is this for a brand team, agency, PR team, research team, or strategy team?
- What budget owner would fund a larger rollout?
- What procurement, legal, or security review would be required after pilot?
- What timeline would matter for a real purchase?

## Disqualifiers

Pause or decline the pilot if:

- The customer requires raw PII or raw CRM data.
- The customer requires live production integration.
- The customer expects guaranteed market predictions.
- The customer will remove source/provenance labels from outputs.
- The customer cannot approve data use.
