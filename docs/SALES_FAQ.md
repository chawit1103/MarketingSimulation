# Sales FAQ

Updated: 2026-05-17

## Is 3C Simulator production SaaS today?

No. It is ready for local demo and is a conditional candidate for controlled private pilots. Public pilot, public internet exposure, and production customer deployment remain blocked.

## What can we sell now?

Sell a guided controlled paid pilot for decision support:

- approved campaign or crisis scenarios,
- no PII,
- no raw CRM records,
- no live integrations,
- source-labeled dashboards and strategy packs,
- workshop interpretation.

## Are the outputs predictions?

No. Outputs are scenario-planning estimates. They should inform decisions and validation plans, not replace market research, legal review, media testing, or executive judgment.

## What data does the first pilot need?

Only approved, non-sensitive campaign context:

- objective,
- target audience,
- market or region,
- channel mix,
- budget range,
- competitor context,
- constraints and risk notes.

## Can a customer upload CRM data?

No for the first pilot. Do not accept raw CRM records, customer lists, PII, support transcripts tied to identifiable people, or regulated personal data.

## Can it connect to live social listening or ad platforms?

No for the first pilot. Live integrations are out of scope until public/production readiness gates are completed and data handling is approved.

## What does Backend Verified mean?

Backend Verified means persisted real simulation KPI/evidence for an owned campaign/run. A successful backend route alone is not enough.

## What is a good first buyer?

Best early buyers:

- agency strategy teams preparing client options,
- brand teams deciding which campaign to revise,
- PR teams stress-testing crisis-sensitive messaging,
- marketing leaders preparing an internal recommendation.

## What should we avoid saying?

Avoid:

- "production-ready",
- "guaranteed prediction",
- "exact ROI",
- "real market truth",
- "fully secure public SaaS",
- "connect your CRM now".

Use:

- "controlled paid pilot",
- "decision-support estimates",
- "scenario planning",
- "source-labeled evidence",
- "validation plan".

## What must be true before public or production use?

The readiness checklist must be completed, including manual credential rotation evidence, production secret operations, shared/edge rate limiting, browser auth hardening or accepted equivalent, data retention policy, and deployment controls.
