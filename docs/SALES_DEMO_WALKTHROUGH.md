# Sales Demo Walkthrough

This walkthrough is for customer-facing controlled paid pilot sales conversations using synthetic/demo data only. It does not claim production readiness, public pilot readiness, public internet readiness, or prediction certainty.

## Required Talk Track Boundaries

Say these clearly in every customer conversation:

- "This is a controlled private pilot candidate."
- "Outputs are decision-support estimates."
- "This does not replace market research."
- "No PII, raw CRM, or live integrations in the first pilot."

Current readiness wording:

- Local demo: ready.
- Controlled private pilot: conditional candidate.
- Public pilot: blocked.
- Public internet exposure: blocked.
- Production customer deployment: blocked.

## 15-Minute Demo Flow

Best for first sales calls, executive screens, and quick qualification.

1. Set context, 1 minute
   - Position 3C Simulator as campaign decision support before real spend.
   - State the readiness and data boundaries above.
2. Campaign pre-test, 3 minutes
   - Open a synthetic demo dashboard, such as Premium Water Launch.
   - Show the source label and confidence/evidence panel.
   - Explain assumptions and limitations.
3. Decision gate, 3 minutes
   - Show KPI summary, decision recommendation, risk level, and recommended validation step.
   - Avoid exact ROI/ROAS or guaranteed outcome language.
4. A/B/C creative comparison, 3 minutes
   - Show comparator route labels and source/provenance.
   - Explain route trade-offs, not certainty.
5. Strategy Pack PPTX export, 3 minutes
   - Show sample deck structure.
   - Point to the provenance footer and limitations slide.
6. Close, 2 minutes
   - Ask whether the prospect has a safe synthetic or approved pilot brief.
   - Confirm first-pilot data boundaries.

## 30-Minute Demo Flow

Best for a qualified buyer or working-session prospect.

1. Set context and data safety, 3 minutes
   - State controlled private pilot candidate status.
   - Confirm synthetic/demo data only for the demo.
2. Campaign pre-test, 5 minutes
   - Show brief quality, dashboard evidence, source labels, and confidence.
3. A/B/C creative comparison, 5 minutes
   - Compare three synthetic creative directions.
   - Discuss which route is strongest and why.
4. Competitor War Room, 5 minutes
   - Show a competitor or crisis scenario.
   - Highlight Live Backend or Demo Mode labels as applicable.
   - If using Local Estimate, make that label explicit.
5. Revised Brief v2, 4 minutes
   - Show how action-plan evidence can revise a campaign brief.
   - Reinforce that the revised brief is a human review input.
6. Budget Scenario Planner, 4 minutes
   - Show assumption-based budget planning.
   - State that it is not exact ROI/ROAS prediction.
7. Strategy Pack PPTX export, 3 minutes
   - Show Brand Executive Summary or Agency Client Pitch Summary structure.
8. Next step, 1 minute
   - Propose pilot discovery, sample brief approval, and data-handling confirmation.

## Marketing Manager / Brand Demo

Buyer goal: reduce launch risk before spending media budget.

Suggested flow:

1. Premium Water Launch or InsurTech Trust Recovery demo brief.
2. Campaign pre-test dashboard.
3. Decision Gate & KPI Summary.
4. Segment Reactions.
5. Risk Drivers & Crisis Watchouts.
6. Revised Brief v2.
7. Budget Scenario Planner.
8. Brand Executive Summary PPTX export.

Suggested talk track:

- "Use this to pressure-test a campaign before full launch."
- "The output gives decision-support estimates and validation priorities."
- "This does not replace research, legal review, or media testing."
- "The first pilot avoids PII, raw CRM, and live integrations."

## Marketing Agency Demo

Buyer goal: compare creative routes and package a client-ready recommendation.

Suggested flow:

1. Agency A/B/C creative direction scenario.
2. A/B/C creative comparison.
3. Segment fit and route trade-offs.
4. Competitor War Room.
5. Recommended Action Plan.
6. Strategy Pack PPTX export as Agency Client Pitch Summary.

Suggested talk track:

- "Use this to compare campaign routes before client presentation."
- "Each route stays source-labeled and limitation-aware."
- "The deck is a pitch aid, not proof of guaranteed market outcome."
- "Client data should be synthetic, anonymized, or explicitly approved for the first pilot."

## Suggested Feature Talk Track

Campaign pre-test:

- "We start by testing the campaign shape against synthetic personas and category context."
- "The goal is to expose risks and segment reactions before real spend."

A/B/C creative comparison:

- "We compare routes as directional decision support."
- "The winner is the strongest simulated route under the stated assumptions."

Competitor War Room:

- "This helps rehearse competitor and crisis pressure."
- "It is a planning simulation, not live market monitoring."

Revised Brief v2:

- "The system turns evidence into a revised brief for human review."
- "The team still owns final approval, claims, and validation."

Budget Scenario Planner:

- "This supports assumption-based planning."
- "It does not provide exact ROI/ROAS prediction."

Strategy Pack PPTX export:

- "The export packages the recommendation, evidence, limitations, and next validation step."
- "Every slide should keep source/provenance visible."

## What Not To Say

Do not say:

- "Production-ready."
- "Ready for public pilot."
- "Guaranteed prediction."
- "Exact ROI."
- "Exact ROAS."
- "Live market truth."
- "No validation needed."
- "Replaces research."

Use:

- "Local demo is ready."
- "Controlled private pilot is a conditional candidate."
- "Public pilot, public internet exposure, and production customer deployment remain blocked."
- "Decision-support estimate, not a guaranteed prediction."
