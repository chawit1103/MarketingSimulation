# Pilot Pricing Packages

Updated: 2026-05-17

These packages are examples for selling controlled paid pilots. They are not production SaaS pricing.

## Pricing Principles

- Price for decision value and guided interpretation, not API usage alone.
- Keep scope narrow: approved briefs, no PII, no live integrations.
- Charge more for executive-ready narrative, workshop time, and additional scenarios.
- Avoid claims of guaranteed ROI or prediction accuracy.

## Package Options

| Package | Best For | Scope | Example Price Range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pilot Starter | One brand or agency team testing value | 1 scenario, 1 dashboard review, 1 action plan, 1 exit call | USD 2,500-5,000 or THB equivalent |
| Strategy Pilot | Brand launch, agency pitch, or PR planning | 2-3 scenarios, comparator or War Room, Strategy Pack PPTX, decision workshop | USD 7,500-15,000 or THB equivalent |
| Executive Pilot | C-level or client-facing decision support | 3-5 scenarios, stakeholder workshop, refined deck, pilot success scorecard | USD 15,000-30,000 or THB equivalent |

Adjust pricing by market, buyer urgency, workshop depth, and whether the work is sold as consulting, software pilot, or hybrid service.

## Add-On Options

- Additional approved scenario.
- Extra stakeholder workshop.
- Agency client pitch pack.
- Competitor/crisis War Room deep dive.
- Post-pilot roadmap and rollout recommendation.

## What Is Not Included

- Production SaaS access.
- Public internet deployment.
- Live provider integration.
- Live CRM, social-listening, or ad-platform integration.
- Custom security review beyond pilot scope.
- Storage of PII or raw customer records.

## Commercial Qualification

Use a paid pilot only when the customer has:

- a real upcoming decision,
- an approved non-sensitive scenario,
- a sponsor who can fund pilot work,
- acceptance that outputs are decision-support estimates,
- willingness to review assumptions and limitations.
