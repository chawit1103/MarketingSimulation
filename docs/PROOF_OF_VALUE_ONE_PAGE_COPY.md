# Proof-of-Value One-Page Copy

Updated: 2026-05-17

## Headline

See how 3C Simulator turns one approved campaign brief into a decision-ready proof-of-value package.

## Subheadline

A guided Proof-of-Value Workshop for marketing, brand, agency, and strategy teams that want clearer campaign decisions before committing spend.

## Who This Is For

This workshop is for teams with one approved campaign brief or historical campaign scenario who need to:

- pressure-test a campaign direction,
- compare A/B/C creative routes,
- identify risk and crisis watchouts,
- improve the brief before launch,
- decide whether a controlled private pilot is worth scoping.

## The Pain Point

Campaign teams often need to choose a direction before the research, media, or post-campaign data is complete. That creates uncertainty around message clarity, audience reaction, risk, and what to validate next.

The Proof-of-Value Workshop gives your team a structured decision-support read using your approved business context.

## What You Provide

- One approved campaign brief or approved historical scenario.
- Objective, target segment, market context, channel plan, budget band, competitor context, risk concerns, and success KPIs.
- Creative directions A/B/C if available.
- Optional approved aggregate actuals if the scenario is historical.

No PII, raw CRM records, customer lists, secrets, or live integrations are required.

## What You Receive

- One-page executive summary.
- Brief Quality Finding.
- Campaign Simulation Summary.
- A/B/C Creative Comparison when creative directions are provided.
- Risk and Crisis Watchouts.
- Revised Brief v2.
- Strategy Pack PPTX outline or export.
- Optional Actual vs Estimate comparison when approved aggregate actuals are provided.
- Recommended Validation Step.
- Limitations and Assumptions.

Outputs include source/provenance labels and are framed as decision-support estimates.

## Session Options

| Option | Best for | Format |

| 30-minute walkthrough | Sales qualification or executive preview | Focused review of brief quality, creative comparison, risks, and next validation step |
| 60-minute workshop | Brand, agency, or strategy team review | Deeper discussion of campaign context, revised brief, Strategy Pack outline, and paid pilot next step |

## Data Boundary

Allowed:

- approved campaign brief or approved historical scenario,
- approved campaign name or anonymized campaign code,
- aggregate-only actuals if approved,
- synthetic/demo examples for explanation.

Not used:

- PII,
- raw CRM records,
- customer lists,
- account-level, household-level, or individual-level records,
- API keys, provider tokens, OAuth credentials, passwords, graph credentials, or secrets,
- live CRM, social-listening, ad-platform, analytics, or marketing automation integrations.

## What Is Excluded

- Production deployment.
- Public pilot access.
- Public internet exposure.
- Guaranteed prediction.
- Exact ROI or ROAS prediction.
- Replacement for market research.
- Live customer-data integrations.

## Short FAQ

**Is this a production deployment?**

No. This is a guided Proof-of-Value Workshop and controlled private pilot candidate.

**Can we use a real campaign brief?**

Yes, if the brief is approved for pilot use and does not include PII, raw CRM records, customer lists, secrets, or live integration credentials.

**Can we include historical performance?**

Yes, only as approved aggregate actuals. The optional Actual vs Estimate comparison is directional and diagnostic.

**Does this predict exact ROI or ROAS?**

No. Outputs are decision-support estimates, not guaranteed performance forecasts.

**Does this replace market research?**

No. The workshop helps clarify what to test, validate, or revise next.

**What happens after the workshop?**

If the output is useful, the next step is a controlled private pilot scoping call with agreed data inputs, success criteria, access controls, and retention/deletion ownership.
