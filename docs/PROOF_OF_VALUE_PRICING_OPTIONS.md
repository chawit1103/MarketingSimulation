# Proof-of-Value Pricing Options

Updated: 2026-05-17

These are example pricing structures for discussing a proof-of-value workshop or package. They are placeholders for sales planning only, not binding quotes.

Pricing should be confirmed separately based on customer scope, operator time, delivery format, data handling requirements, and whether a controlled paid pilot follows.

## Positioning

The proof-of-value package is a guided workshop/session and decision-support package. It is not production SaaS, not public pilot access, and not guaranteed campaign forecasting.

## Example Options

| Option | Example range | Includes | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Intro Proof-of-Value Session | Placeholder range: USD 1,500-3,000 | 30-minute walkthrough, one approved brief, high-level output review, recommended validation step | Best for sales qualification and budget-owner interest. |
| Standard Proof-of-Value Package | Placeholder range: USD 3,000-7,500 | Intake review, 60-minute workshop, output package, Strategy Pack outline or export, follow-up summary | Best for a team comparing creative routes. |
| Paid Pilot Scoping Add-On | Placeholder range: USD 2,500-5,000 | Pilot scope, data boundary, success criteria, retention/deletion plan, operator checklist | Applies when customer wants to move toward a controlled paid pilot. |

## Pricing Assumptions

- One approved campaign brief or historical scenario.
- No PII.
- No raw CRM records.
- No customer lists.
- No secrets.
- No live integrations.
- Optional actuals are aggregate-only and approved.
- Outputs are decision-support estimates.

## Not Included

- Production deployment.
- Public pilot access.
- Public internet exposure.
- Live CRM, social-listening, ad-platform, analytics, or marketing automation integrations.
- Guaranteed prediction.
- Exact ROI or ROAS prediction.
- Replacement for market research.

## Commercial Next Step

Before quoting, confirm:

- customer objective,
- approved brief availability,
- required stakeholders,
- output package format,
- data-handling constraints,
- desired session length,
- whether a controlled paid pilot is being considered.
