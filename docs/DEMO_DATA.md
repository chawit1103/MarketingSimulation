## Brief 1: Premium Water Launch

**Campaign name:** Premium Water Launch TH

**Objective:** Product launch and brand perception.

**Audience:** Urban health-conscious buyers, young professionals, and family shoppers in Bangkok, Chiang Mai, and major retail areas.

**Duration:** 8 weeks.

**Budget range:** THB 2M-3M media spend.

**Primary KPI:** Brand lift, message resonance, and trial intent.

**Channel mix:** Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LINE, Twitter/X.

**Competitor context:** Discount bottled-water brands may frame the product as overpriced. Wellness brands may compete on purity and sustainability.

**Brand constraints:** Premium but practical tone. Avoid medical or disease-prevention claims.

**Risk/legal notes:** Sustainability and mineral-benefit claims need legal review before launch.

**Demo angle:** Best for showing the no-key demo dashboard, Confidence & Evidence, and Action Plan.

## Brief 2: InsurTech Claim App

**Campaign name:** Fast Claim App Relaunch

**Objective:** Conversion and trust repair.

**Audience:** Thai insurance customers aged 25-55, including families, SME owners, and people who recently filed claims.

**Duration:** 6 weeks.

**Budget range:** THB 1M-2M.

**Primary KPI:** App activation, trust sentiment, and reduction in support objections.

**Channel mix:** Facebook, LINE, YouTube, TikTok.

**Competitor context:** Traditional insurers emphasize branch support. Digital challengers emphasize speed and convenience.

**Brand constraints:** Avoid implying every claim is approved instantly. Use proof, service-level clarity, and customer education.

**Risk/legal notes:** Product terms, claim exclusions, and privacy language must be reviewed.

**Demo angle:** Good for Brief Quality Score and risk-driver discussion.

## Brief 3: EV Test Drive Weekend

**Campaign name:** EV Test Drive Weekend

**Objective:** Lead generation and competitor response.

**Audience:** Upper-middle-income commuters, fleet buyers, and first-time EV considerers in Bangkok and nearby provinces.

**Duration:** 4 weeks.

**Budget range:** THB 3M-5M.

**Primary KPI:** Test-drive booking rate and qualified leads.

**Channel mix:** Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn.

**Competitor context:** Competitors may respond with price cuts, charging-station claims, or warranty comparisons.

**Brand constraints:** Do not exaggerate range, charging time, battery warranty, or total cost savings.

**Risk/legal notes:** Financing terms and technical claims require approval.

**Demo angle:** Good for War Room competitor launch and price-war scenarios.

## Brief 4: Hospital Preventive Checkup

**Campaign name:** Preventive Health Package

**Objective:** Awareness and appointment conversion.

**Audience:** Working adults, caregivers, and corporate HR teams in urban Thailand.

**Duration:** 10 weeks.

**Budget range:** THB 1.5M-2.5M.

**Primary KPI:** Appointment inquiries, lead quality, and trust sentiment.

**Channel mix:** Facebook, LINE, YouTube, LinkedIn.

**Competitor context:** Other hospitals may compete on price, doctor reputation, or package bundles.

**Demo angle:** Good for crisis-prevention and regulatory-risk discussion.

## Brief 5: Restaurant Franchise Promo

**Objective:** Foot traffic and franchise interest.

**Duration:** 5 weeks.
